Calculation logic

Read-only. Calculation methodology is controlled and cannot be modified from the application.

Gross difference

Gross Difference Qty = Physical Qty + Negative Qty + QTY Sold − Final System Qty
Gross Difference Value = Gross Difference Qty × Article MRP

Negative inventory

Negative quantity is added into the gross difference. System 0, physical 0, negative 1 gives +1.

In-transit — evaluated at STN level only

For each STN:
  positive-GD articles ÷ total articles in STN × 100
  STN qualifies only when the result is strictly greater than 50%
  Qualified   → all quantities of that STN are included
  Not qualified → zero in-transit quantity, even for a positive-GD article
Exactly 50% does not qualify.

OMNI returns

OMNI Qty Considered = min(OMNI file qty, Gross Difference Qty) when GD > 0, otherwise 0

Sales data

Optional. When absent, QTY Sold is zero. Sales quantities are never estimated or inferred from other files.

MRP

Book Stock MRP, then Physical Stock MRP, then Article Master MRP. Unresolved MRP is flagged as MRP NOT FOUND — never estimated, averaged or borrowed from another article.

Final variance

Final Difference Qty = Gross Difference Qty − In-Transit Qty − OMNI Return Qty
Final Difference Value = Final Difference Qty × Article MRP
