Static vs. dynamic QR codes

    

      

        
Static QR (used by CodeForge)

        
Data is encoded directly inside the QR pattern. Works without a server, no subscription, no database — the code will scan the same forever.

      

      

        
Dynamic QR

        
The QR stores a short redirect URL (e.g. https://yourdomain.com/a/AST-000124) whose destination can change later. This requires a backend/database to host and update the redirect — CodeForge can generate the QR for such a URL, but it cannot host the redirect itself.

🔒 Privacy first

    
Barcode and QR generation happens locally in your browser. Location access requires your explicit permission and is only requested when you click a location button — never automatically. Your data is not uploaded to any server unless you explicitly use a server-based feature (CodeForge has none). History, batch data and preferences are stored only in this browser's local storage.
